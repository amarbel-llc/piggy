piggy spike — WebUSB → PIV slot-9D ECDH

What this proves (hardware half of the spike): that the browser can talk CCID over WebUSB to a YubiKey, run GENERAL AUTHENTICATE (ECDH) on PIV slot 9D, and recover a shared secret Z that matches the OpenSSL-derived / RustCrypto-derived value the decrypt half already verified.

Caveats: WebUSB is Chromium-only. The OS smartcard service (pcscd / Windows Smart Card service) may already hold the CCID interface — if claimInterface fails with "device busy", stop pcscd (Linux: sudo systemctl stop pcscd pcscd.socket) or unbind the reader first. This harness is untested on hardware from this environment; treat APDU quirks (PIN/touch policy on 9D, extended-length) as expected spike friction.

Step 1 — get the ephemeral point from a box addressed to your card

Encrypt anything to your card, then run:
cargo run --example open -- --box <ebox-hex>
Paste its ephemeral_pubkey (uncompressed, starts with 04) below and pick the curve.
